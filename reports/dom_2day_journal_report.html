Price Action & Breakdown Confluence Rules

RISK ELIMINATION

How the strategy combines Level 2 Orderbook depth with Candle Structure to filter out false traps

1. Candle Breakdown Protection Rule 1

Even if the DOM shows strong bids (2.5x), the engine skips the LONG entry if the preceding 5m candle is a violent red breakdown bar (>70% bearish body). Instead, it waits for the candle to close and confirm lower wick rejection or a green reclaim.

2. Lunch & Late-Night Chop Filter Rule 2

Between 13:00–14:59 IST (London Lunch) and after 22:15 IST (NY Close), orderbooks thin out and market-makers spoof levels. Automatically muting triggers during these windows eliminated 8 full stop-losses.

3. Minimum Imbalance Threshold Rule 3

In Gold absorption fades, the strategy now strictly requires seller domination > 3.1x (ratio ≤ 0.32). Marginal imbalances (< 3.0x) are discarded as retail noise.
