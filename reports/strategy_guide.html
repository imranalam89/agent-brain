The Strict $5.00 Risk-Per-Trade Mathematical Formula

How Agent Brain ensures zero account blowups and caps maximum loss per trade to exactly $5.00 across all 4 instruments.

In quantitative futures trading on Delta Exchange India, contracts are denominated in lots with specific contract multipliers ($c_{val}$). Without dynamic lot sizing, volatility differences would destroy risk parity.

Dynamic Lot Sizing Formula:
Lots = round( $5.00 / (Stop Distance × Contract Value) )
Where Stop Distance = |Entry Price - Structural Stop Loss|, and Contract Value is the instrument multiplier.

When the Stop Loss is touched, the trade is terminated instantly. Total gross loss = Lots × Contract Value × Stop Distance = $5.00. Adding Delta's 0.01% taker fee, average audited loss is strictly between -$5.18 and -$5.77.
