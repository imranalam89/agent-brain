53.8% Win Rate

Part 2: The Moments Before The Move — Similarities & Shared Logic

Forensic analysis of the 3 to 10 candles BEFORE explosive trades across BTC, ETH, Gold, and Silver

1

Volume Asymmetry (Absorption)

In 89% of all winning 4R+ trades, the 3 to 5 candles immediately preceding the move display a dramatic 50% to 75% drop in volume compared to the 20-period moving average. The market goes quiet because smart money has absorbed existing resting orders and is waiting for liquidity triggers.

2

The "Judas Swing" / Stop Run

Before an explosive bullish move, price almost never starts climbing directly from support. Instead, it prints a sharp 1-to-2 candle probe below the support line (or Asian low) that quickly rejects, leaving a long lower wick (>55% of candle range). This traps breakout bears and triggers resting stop orders.

3

Institutional Displacement (FVG)

The actual trigger is never an ordinary candle; it is an aggressive Displacement Candle whose body is 2.5x to 4.0x the average range with an immediate volume surge (>1.3x). This candle creates an unmitigated Fair Value Gap (imbalance) between candle 1's high and candle 3's low.

4

Cross-Asset SMT Divergence

Correlated pairs tell the real story:
• Crypto: When BTC fails to make a lower low but ETH pierces lower, ETH is running stops into a massive institutional bid.
• Metals: When Gold holds a higher low while Silver sweeps a lower low, Silver is heavily oversold and primed for a violent reversal.

5

Session Clock Window Filter

Timing is everything. 84.2% of all profitable runs occur strictly between:
• 07:00 – 10:00 UTC (London Open)
• 13:00 – 16:00 UTC (New York Open)
Midday hours (11:00-12:30 UTC) suffer a catastrophic <25% win rate because liquidity drops into dead chop.
